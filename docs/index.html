Why I Made Repertoire

It started with the keyboard.

After learning to play the keyboard for 8 years and mastering hundreds of songs, I had a sudden realization: I could only play about 5 to 8 songs off the top of my head.

I had forgotten almost everything else. If I wanted to play those older pieces again, I would have to dig through old physical notes, scour the internet for sheet music I once had, and try to piece it all together. To make matters worse, some of my older notebooks were lost entirely.

"I needed a single, reliable place to store all my learnings, materials, PDFs, audio tracks, and setups, without the fear of losing them."

Then, it happened again.

As I started learning magic, I ran into the exact same problem. There are countless magic tricks out there that have similar effects but use completely different methods. I found myself unable to keep track of all the sources, where I had originally learned a trick, the specific setups required, and the subtle nuances of the performances.

That's when I realized this wasn't just a music problem. It's a problem for anyone who builds a repertoire of skills. That's why I built this app—to give performers of all kinds a dedicated space to track their growth and preserve their hard-earned repertoire.

Powerful Features

📚 Repertoire Library

View your entire collection in a beautiful grid or list format. Organize pieces with custom groups and tags for easy access.

🎵 Detailed Piece View

Each piece has a dedicated page with comprehensive metadata including title, composer, difficulty, and custom tags.

📎 Flexible Media Attachments

Attach PDFs, Markdown notes, images, audio files, and video links. Keep all your sheet music, tutorials, and performances in one place.

⏱️ Practice Tracking

Log practice sessions, track dates, and monitor your progress with detailed statistics and history.

🔍 Smart Search & Filtering

Full-text search with advanced filtering by genre, difficulty, tags, practice history, and more.

💾 Backup & Restore

Export your entire library for safekeeping. Automatic periodic backups ensure your data is always protected.
